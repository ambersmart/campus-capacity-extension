Fill % = average attendance ÷ auditorium seats for the window above. A service turns red once it hits its "full" line: 70% for prime Sunday-morning hours (was 80%), 40% for every other service.

A prime service that has stayed at or above 70% for 4 of the last 5 weeks is flagged as a growth trigger — the signal to plan a new service for the next growth season (fall, January, Easter).

By campus

Total seats counts one service per campus (the auditorium's adult seating). Prime hours are services that start between 9:00 and 11:00am on Sunday. These are today's baseline numbers — the starting point for the "do we have plans to add seats / prime hours / services in the next 6–12 months?" conversation. If a campus's seat count here includes rooms you would not count (like the DDC balconies), adjust Capacity Auditorium in the data for that campus and it flows through everywhere.
